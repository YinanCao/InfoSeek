You are about to participate in the research study entitled: Colour Perception and Learning

Researchers in charge: Dr. Valentin Wyart and Dr. Yinan Cao at École Normale Supérieure in Paris, France

This study aims to investigate the cognitive mechanisms of learning and decision-making. The proposed experiment has no immediate application or clinical value, but it will allow us to improve our understanding of the functioning brain. We are asking you to participate in this study because you have been recruited by the RISC or Prolific platform.

PROCEDURE

In this study, you will complete a simple learning task. No special skills are required, as long as you are not colour-blind. The study takes about 60 minutes to finish.

VOLUNTARY PARTICIPATION AND CONFIDENTIALITY

Your participation in this study is voluntary. This means that you are consenting to participate in this project without external pressure. During your participation, the researchers in charge will collect and record information about you. In order to protect your identity and the confidentiality of the data, the identification of each file will be coded, thus preserving the anonymity of your answers. We will not collect any personal data from the RISC or Prolific platform. The researchers in charge of this study will only use the data for research purposes in order to achieve the scientific objectives of the project. The data may be published in scientific journals, in which case no publication or scientific communication will contain identifiable information.
